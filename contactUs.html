A QUICK INTRODUCTION

A little head-scratching.
A lot of aha.

  1. Read between the lines.

    There’s one riddle each day. Take your time; there’s no clock to beat.

  2. You have three guesses.

    Type an answer and press Enter or Check answer. Only a wrong answer uses an attempt.

  3. Need a little nudge?

    Reveal the hint whenever you like. It won’t cost you a guess.

  4. Make it a daily ritual.

    Finish a riddle to build your play streak, whether you solve it or use all three guesses. The next riddle arrives at midnight, your local time.
